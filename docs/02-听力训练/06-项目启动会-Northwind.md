# 听力06 · 项目启动会（Northwind）

> 场景：kickoff call（美音）｜角色：Tom，Northwind PM
> 主题：三个月时间线、里程碑、GitHub 协作、Jira、预算
> 音频：audio/06.mp3（仓库内文件，可在线播放）

## 原文脚本

```
Good morning, everyone, and welcome to the kickoff call for the data
pipeline project. I am Tom, the project manager at Northwind. Let me
run through the plan quickly. We have agreed on a three-month timeline,
starting Monday. Milestone one is the data ingestion module, due in
four weeks; milestone two, the analytics dashboard, due in eight weeks;
and the final milestone, production deployment, in twelve weeks. We
will have a demo at the end of each milestone. On communication: we
will have a weekly call on Wednesdays, at nine a.m. Eastern, and you
can reach me on Slack any time. For the codebase, we use GitHub, and
the branch strategy is main plus feature branches. Every pull request
needs one approval before merge, and we run automated tests on every
push. We will track progress in Jira, and you will update your tickets
daily. Finally, the budget is fifty thousand dollars for the full
scope, with twenty percent paid upfront and the rest on milestone
completion. Any questions?
```

## 本课批改要点

- **错数**：21 处（较听力05 回落，技术词训练见效）
- **GitHub/Jira/pull request/merge/push 全对**——听力05 的技术词训练直接见效
- **8 个数字 7 个对**：4/8/12 weeks、9 a.m.、20% 全对；唯一错点 fifty thousand → "15 thousand"
- **新暴露 = 词义级错误**：deployment（部署）→ "development"、due in → "doing"、automated tests → "all the made test"

## 本课必记

1. **到期/截止**：**due in** four weeks（不是 doing）
2. **仪表盘 / 部署**：analytics **dashboard**、production **deployment**（部署 ≠ development 开发）
3. **自动化测试**：**automated tests**（注意弱读：听成 "all the made test" 是整块塌陷）
4. **更新工单**：update your **tickets** daily
5. **预算数字**：fifty thousand dollars = **50K**（听成 15K 说明 50/15 仍是软肋）
