# 听力03 · 财报电话会（Q2 业绩）

> 场景：财报电话会 prepared remarks（女声美音，语速最快、数字最密）
> 音频：https://aka.doubaocdn.com/s/UJUlcwKGbr

## 原文脚本

```
Good morning, everyone, and thank you for joining today's call. I'll
start with the key numbers for the second quarter. Revenue came in at
forty-eight point six million dollars, up twelve percent year over year,
slightly above the midpoint of our guidance. Net income was seven point
two million, with a net margin of fourteen point eight percent. Our
recurring revenue, which is now fifty-five percent of total revenue,
grew twenty-one percent compared to the same period last year. On the
cost side, operating expenses increased eleven percent, mostly due to
hiring in our engineering team. Looking ahead, we are raising our
full-year guidance to two hundred million dollars, and we expect
third-quarter revenue between forty-nine and fifty-one million. Finally,
we're announcing a share buyback of ten million dollars, to be completed
by the end of the fourth quarter. That concludes our prepared remarks;
we'll now open the line for questions.
```

## 本课批改要点

- **错数**：14 处
- **三处 teen/ty 数字陷阱全错**：14.8 → "40.8"、55% → "15%"、51 → "50"（→ 触发专题：teen/ty）
- **词块未激活**：recurring revenue → "current revenue"、full-year guidance → "four years gandents"、share buyback → "share buy by"

## 本课必记

1. **teen/ty 重音规律**：-teen 重音在后（four-TEEN）、-ty 重音在前（FOR-ty），详见专题
2. **同比**：**year over year**（YoY），不是 year of year
3. **经常性收入**：**recurring revenue**（订阅/经常性，不是 current revenue）
4. **全年指引**：**full-year guidance**；**股份回购**：**share buyback**
5. **数字双保险**：听到数字先按常识校验——净利率 40% 偏高、经常性收入占比 15% 偏低，不对劲就重听
