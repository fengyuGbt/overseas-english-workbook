# 邮件01 · 回复报价谈判

> 训练日期：2026-09-14｜对应听力04（Mark / Horizon Data 报价谈判）
> 场景：客户接受技术方案但要求：①9000→7500，条件 50% 预付 ②第一里程碑 4 周 ③每周英文进度报告 ④周五签合同、周一开工

## 场景任务

假设你就是开发者，接受这些条款，写一封回复邮件（80-120 词），包含 6 件套：

1. Subject　2. 称呼+感谢+接受　3. 逐条确认条款　4. 确认进度报告　5. 提 1-2 个问题　6. 收尾+签名

## 逐句批改

| 你写的 | 批注 |
|---|---|
| **i** would be happy to proceed on those terms you mentioned | I 大写；"you mentioned" 冗余可删 |
| furthermore, **i** would like to confirm **those term** **by** this email | I；term→**terms**；by→**in** |
| **i** can accept $7500 for **full scope** with 50% paid **unfront** | I；**$7,500**；the full scope；**upfront**（第二次错） |
| **i** can **finish** the data ingestion module in four weeks | I；finish→**deliver**（交付） |
| **also i** will send weekly progress reports in English | Also, I...（内容✓） |
| could you **comfirm** **the detail** about SOW, **may i** have **right to interview data**? | confirm；details；May I；**access to the data**（interview 是面试≠访问） |
| what is the standard for delivery. | 问号；更专业：**What are the acceptance criteria?** |
| if **anythiny** is ok, we can sign the **constract**... | anything；looks good / is in order；contract |
| **Looking forward with you.** | **Looking forward to working with you.**（第二次错） |

**错误统计**：16 处 = 大小写 6 + 拼写 4（upfront/confirm/contract/anything）+ 介词搭配 2 + 单复数 2 + 词义 2

## 标准范文

```
Subject: Re: Proposal for the Market Data Pipeline

Hi Mark,

Thank you for your email. I'd be happy to proceed on those terms. To confirm the details:

1. Fee: $7,500 for the full scope, with 50% paid upfront.
2. Timeline: the data ingestion module will be delivered within four weeks.
3. Reporting: weekly progress reports in English.

Before we sign, could you confirm the following?
- Will I have access to the exchange data feed for development and testing?
- What are the acceptance criteria for each milestone?

If everything looks good, we can sign the contract by Friday and start on Monday.

Looking forward to working with you.

Best regards,
FF
```

## 本课必记

1. **upfront**（预付）、**access to**（访问权限）、**acceptance criteria**（验收标准）
2. **look forward to + 动名词**——写结尾前自查
3. **提问三件套**：could you confirm: ① ② ③——编号列表，别用逗号连串
