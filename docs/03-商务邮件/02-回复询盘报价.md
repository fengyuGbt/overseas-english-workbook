# 邮件02 · 回复询盘 + 报价

> 训练日期：2026-09-14｜对应听力01（Daniel Park / QuantEdge Analytics 询盘）
> 场景：客户留言问两件事——①下个月可用时间 ②基于范围的粗略报价

## 场景任务

你是开发者，回复 Daniel：给出下个月可用时间（前两周可用、每周 40 小时）+ 时薪报价。用 6 件套结构，80-120 词。

## 你的习作（原样）

```
Hi Daniel
Thank you for your email. I would like to confirm your two concerns:
1. The first and second week of next are available for me. I can work for 40 hours a week.
2. $65 a hour is my quote.
Looking forward for hearing from you.
Best regards,ff
```

## 逐句批改

| 你写的 | 批注 |
|---|---|
| I would like to confirm your two **concerns** | concerns 是"担忧"→ **questions / requests** |
| The first and second **week** **of next** are available for me | **weeks**；**of next month**——next 不能裸用 |
| I can work for 40 hours a week. | ✓ 全对 |
| **$65 a hour** is my quote | **an hour**（h 不发音）；更地道：**My hourly rate is $65.** |
| Looking forward **for** hearing from you | **to**（顽固点，第三次错） |
| Best regards,**ff** | 签名大写：**FF** |

**错误统计**：7 处 = 语法残缺 1（of next）+ 单复数 1 + 冠词 1（a/an）+ 介词 1（for/to）+ 用词 2（concerns、is my quote）+ 大小写 1（ff）

**进步**：编号列表用上（课01 三件套）；hearing from you 拼对；40 hours a week 全对；总错数 16→7。

## 标准范文

```
Subject: Re: Availability and Quote

Hi Daniel,

Thank you for your email. To answer your two questions:

1. Availability: I am available for the first two weeks of next month, and I can work 40 hours a week.
2. Quote: my hourly rate is $65.

I can also share a detailed breakdown based on the scope you sent if that would help.

Looking forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,
FF
```

## 本课必记

1. **look forward to + doing**——第三次错，写邮件结尾前强制自查这一句
2. **an hour**（h 不发音）；同类：an honest answer、an hourly rate
3. **of next month**——next 后面必须跟时间单位（month / week / Monday）

## 顽固点专题：look forward to（三连错记录）

| 轮次 | 写法 | 错误 |
|---|---|---|
| 听力01 听写 | look forward **to hear** from you | 缺动名词 |
| 邮件01 | Looking forward **with** you | 介词错 |
| 邮件02 | Looking forward **for** hearing from you | 介词错 |

> 每次错法不同 = 固定搭配没进记忆。**to 后面永远跟动名词**：hearing / working / working with you。
