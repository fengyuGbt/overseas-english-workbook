# 外贸02 · 报价与还价（Quotation & Negotiation）

> 场景：买家说"太贵了"，要求降价
> 目标：守住利润底线地让步，把"降价"谈成"换条件"

## 一、报价单要素（缺一不可）

一份专业报价必须写清：

| 要素 | 英文 | 备注 |
|---|---|---|
| 单价 | unit price | 写明币种 USD / EUR |
| 贸易术语 | Incoterms（FOB / CIF / DDP…） | 见外贸03 |
| 数量阶梯 | price breaks by quantity | 量越大价越低 |
| 有效期 | quotation valid for 15 days | 防材料涨价 |
| 付款方式 | payment terms | T/T、L/C、部分预付 |
| 交期 | lead time | 下单后 X 天 |

## 二、还价攻防高频句

**对方压价时你会听到：**
- Your price is **too high** compared with other suppliers.
- We have a better offer from your competitor.
- Can you **give us a discount** for a bulk order?
- Could you **meet us halfway**? / Split the difference?

**你的回应套路（先认同→再换条件）：**
- I understand your concern about the price.
- Our price is **based on** the current material cost.
- **If you increase the order to** [数量], we can offer you [X]% off.
- We can **reduce the price by** [X]% **if** you pay 50% upfront.
- The best I can do is [X]. This is our **bottom line**.

## 三、让步三原则（谈不崩的秘诀）

1. **永远不白降**：每降一分钱，换一个条件（更大订单 / 更短账期 / 预付比例提高）。
2. **降价给锚点**：报价时留 3-8% 空间，还价有得让。
3. **书面确认**：口头谈好的价格，邮件复述确认再发正式 PI（形式发票）。

## 四、谈判邮件模板

```
Subject: Revised Quotation — [型号]

Dear [Name],

Thank you for your feedback on our quotation. We understand that 
price is an important factor for you.

We have carefully reviewed the cost structure. As a special 
arrangement for your first order, we can offer:

- Unit price: USD [X] (original USD [Y])
- Condition: order quantity of [数量] or above, and 50% upfront payment

This is the best price we can offer while keeping our quality 
and delivery standard. We believe you will find it competitive.

If this works for you, we will send the PI (Proforma Invoice) 
today so we can start production as soon as possible.

Best regards,
[你的名字]
```

## 五、易错点

1. **"便宜一点"**：不要说 cheap（贬义），说 **a better price / a discount**。
2. **"降价 X%"**：**reduce the price by** X%（by 表幅度），不是 "reduce X%"。
3. **"最低价"**：**the best price we can offer / our bottom line**。书面慎用 bottom line（太硬），口头谈价可用。
4. **"在成本基础上"**：based **on** the cost，不是 based with。
5. **数量折扣**：**bulk order discount / volume discount**，别直译 "number discount"。

## 六、小练习

1. 翻译：如果你把订单量提高到 1000 件，我们可以给你 5% 的折扣。
2. 买家说 "Your competitor quoted us 10% less."，你第一句怎么回？（写出来）
3. 选词：The best I can do **is / are** USD 8.5 per piece.
