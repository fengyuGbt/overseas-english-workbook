# 外贸04 · 交期、验货与催款（Delivery, Inspection & Payment Follow-up）

> 场景：订单在生产/运输中，交期变了、客户要验货、尾款该催了
> 目标：交期变更主动沟通、验货安排清晰、催款不失礼

## 一、交期沟通

**主动报进度（好习惯）：**
- We would like to **update you on the production progress**.
- Production is **on schedule**; the goods will be ready by [日期].
- We will **keep you posted** once the goods are shipped.

**交期延迟——必须先开口：**
- Unfortunately, we have to **inform you of a delay** in production.
- Due to [原因: raw material shortage / capacity issues], the shipment
  will be delayed **by about [X] days**.
- We apologize for the inconvenience and will do our best to
  **expedite the delivery**.

> 规则：宁可早说，不可瞒报。延迟 1 天先说，比到期才说强 10 倍。

## 二、验货安排（Inspection）

**常见三种：**
1. **Pre-shipment inspection (PSI)**：发货前验货
2. **Third-party inspection**：第三方验货（SGS、BV、TÜV）
3. **Sample approval first**：先确认样品再生产大货

**高频句：**
- Please arrange a **pre-shipment inspection** before loading.
- We have arranged **third-party inspection** by SGS on [日期].
- The **inspection report** will be sent to you within 24 hours.
- **Any defective items** found will be replaced at our cost.

## 三、催款（Payment Follow-up）

催款三阶梯（先软后硬），每封留好记录：

**第一封 · 温和提醒（到期前/刚到期）：**
```
Subject: Invoice [编号] — Payment Reminder

Dear [Name],

This is a gentle reminder that the invoice [编号] of 
USD [金额] is due on [日期]. 

If the payment has already been arranged, please ignore this email. 
Kindly confirm the expected date of transfer so we can arrange 
the shipment accordingly.

Best regards,
[你的名字]
```

**第二封 · 明确要求（逾期 7-14 天）：**
```
Subject: Overdue Payment — Invoice [编号]

Dear [Name],

Our records show that the payment for invoice [编号] 
(USD [金额]) is now [X] days overdue.

We would appreciate it if you could arrange the transfer 
by [日期]. Please note that production/delivery of your next 
order may be affected until this is settled.

Please let us know if there is any issue with the payment.
```

**第三封 · 最后通牒（逾期 30 天+，书面留痕）：**
- 明确说 **we will have to suspend further shipments / stop production**，必要时走平台仲裁（Trade Assurance claim）或法律途径。**所有沟通留邮件记录。**

## 四、易错点

1. **"催款"**：**follow up on the payment / send a payment reminder**。别写 "urge you to pay"（太冲，容易毁关系）。
2. **"到期"**：**due**——The invoice is due on... / The balance is due before shipment.
3. **"逾期"**：**overdue**（The payment is 10 days overdue.）
4. **"尽快"**：**at your earliest convenience**（商务客套）或 **as soon as possible**。
5. **"发货前"**：**before shipment / prior to shipment**；别写 "before deliver"。

## 五、小练习

1. 翻译：很遗憾通知您，由于原材料短缺，交期将延迟约 7 天。
2. 客户尾款逾期 10 天，写第一句催款邮件开头（温和版）。
3. 选词：The goods will be ready **on / in** schedule.
