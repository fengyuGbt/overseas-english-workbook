# 外贸11 · 样品开发与打样（Sampling & Prototyping）

> 场景：开发阶段，客户要样品——几个样件？样品费怎么算？先付还是后付？
> 目标：样品环节的钱、数量、责任边界一次说清，不白干也不得罪客户

## 一、样品类型（先分清是哪一种样）

| 样品 | 英文 | 用途 | 收费逻辑 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 工程样 | Engineering Sample | 验证功能/结构 | 通常收费 |
| 确认样 | Approval Sample / Pre-production Sample | 客户确认外观/手感，量产基准 | 收费或免费 |
| 金样/封样 | Golden Sample / Sealed Sample | 双方签封，验收对照 | 随大货走 |
| 测试样 | Test Sample | 客户做测试认证 | 按测试需求收费 |

## 二、样品数量（几个样件合理）

**判断依据：**
- **功能测试**：一般 2-5 件（破坏性测试要多备）
- **外观确认**：1-2 件足够
- **客户内部评审/测试**：按客户要求，常见 3-5 件
- **模具试模件**：随模具数量，一般 3-5 套

**话术：**
- We can provide **up to [X] samples** free of charge.
- Additional samples beyond [X] pcs will be **charged at cost**.
- For destructive testing, we suggest [X] pieces.

## 三、样品费怎么定义（核心三问）

1. **模具/工装费（tooling cost）**：新开模必须收（几万到几十万不等），可谈"下单返还"。
2. **样品材料人工费（sample cost）**：小件可免，大件/定制收成本价。
3. **运费（shipping cost）**：默认 **freight collect（到付）** 或客户账户到付（DHL/FedEx account）。

**收费模式（写进邮件）：**
- 模具费：100% 预付，**首单量产后返还**（tooling cost will be refunded after the first order）。
- 样品费：**先付款后寄样**（samples are payable in advance）。
- 样品费可抵扣首单（sample fee is **deductible from the first bulk order**）。

## 四、样品环节邮件模板

### 确认样品费与付款

```
Subject: Sample Order — [产品/型号]

Dear [Name],

Regarding your sample request, here is our confirmation:

- Samples: [X] pcs
- Sample cost: USD [X] (free of charge if [条件])
- Tooling cost: USD [X], refundable after the first order
- Shipping: via DHL, freight collect / to your account
- Payment: sample fee to be paid in advance by T/T

Once we receive the payment, we will start the samples and 
deliver within [X] days.

Best regards,
[你的名字]
```

### 寄样通知

```
Subject: Samples Shipped — Tracking No.

Dear [Name],

We are pleased to inform you that the samples of [产品/型号] 
were shipped on [日期] via DHL.

- Tracking number: [单号]
- Estimated delivery: [日期]

Please check the samples upon arrival and let us know your 
feedback, especially on [外观/功能/颜色]. We will make 
adjustments if needed.

Best regards,
[你的名字]
```

## 五、易错点

1. **"打样"**：**make samples / produce samples**；"寄样" = send samples。别用 "make a model"（那是做模型）。
2. **"样品费"**：**sample cost / sample charge**；"免费样品" = free samples。
3. **"到付"**：**freight collect**（收件方付）；"寄方付" = freight prepaid。搞反会闹误会。
4. **"抵扣"**：**deductible from**（The sample fee is deductible from the bulk order.）
5. **"先付款后发货"**：payable **in advance** / payment **before shipment**。

## 六、小练习

1. 翻译：样品费将在首单中抵扣，模具费 100% 预付并随首单返还。
2. 客户说 "Can you send us free samples? We will place a big order later."，你怎么回才既专业又不白送？（写 2-3 句）
3. 选词：Samples will be shipped **freight collect / collected**.
