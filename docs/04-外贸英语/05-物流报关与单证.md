# 外贸05 · 物流、报关与单证（Logistics, Customs & Documents）

> 场景：货要发走了——订舱、报关、制单、通知客户
> 目标：单证名称不出错、物流节点能沟通、清关问题会应对

## 一、核心单证清单（一张表记牢）

| 单证 | 英文全称 | 作用 |
|---|---|---|
| **商业发票** | Commercial Invoice | 货款结算 + 报关价值依据 |
| **装箱单** | Packing List | 件数、毛重/净重、体积 |
| **提单** | Bill of Lading (B/L) | 物权凭证！凭此提货 |
| **空运单** | Air Waybill (AWB) | 空运的"提单"（非物权凭证） |
| **原产地证** | Certificate of Origin (C/O) | 关税优惠/产地证明 |
| **熏蒸证明** | Fumigation Certificate | 木包装出口美国等必备 |
| **保险单** | Insurance Policy | CIF/CIP 时必须 |
| **报关单** | Customs Declaration | 出口报关用 |

## 二、物流节点高频句

**发货通知（shipping notice）：**
- The goods have been **shipped** on [日期] **via** [船公司/快递].
- The estimated **arrival date (ETA)** is [日期].
- We will send you the **tracking number** once available.

**客户问货到哪了：**
- Your order is now **in transit** / **at the port of loading** / **awaiting customs clearance**.
- There is a slight **delay at customs** due to [原因]; we are following up with the forwarder.

**物流常用词：**
- **freight forwarder** 货代 ｜ **container**（20GP / 40HQ）集装箱
- **sea freight / air freight / express**（DHL、FedEx、UPS）海运/空运/快递
- **demurrage** 滞箱费 ｜ **detention** 用箱超期费 ｜ **transit time** 运输时间

## 三、报关与清关应对

**出口报关（你方）：**
- We are **arranging the customs declaration** for your order.
- Please confirm the **HS code** and the declared value.

**进口清关（客户方/目的港）：**
- The shipment is **held at customs** in [国家] because of missing documents.
- Please provide the **Power of Attorney (POA)** so our broker can clear the goods.
- The goods were **released** from customs on [日期].

> 常见清关卡点：**木包装没熏蒸证明**、**申报价值与实际不符**、**HS code 归类错**。发货前跟客户确认清关文件要求。

## 四、易错点

1. **"提单"必须会拼**：**Bill of Lading**（Lading 不是 Loading！）。缩写 B/L。
2. **"毛重/净重"**：**gross weight / net weight**（gross 不是 total）。
3. **"已发货"**：**shipped**（The goods have been shipped.）别写 "sent out the goods" 也行，但 shipped 是标准。
4. **"在运输中"**：**in transit**，不是 "on the way" 的正式版——in transit 是商务标准。
5. **"通知对方"**：**keep you informed / notify you**；We will keep you informed of the shipping status.

## 五、小练习

1. 翻译：货物已于 9 月 20 日装船，预计 10 月 5 日抵达洛杉矶港。
2. 客户在目的港清关被卡，说要 missing documents，你第一反应该问什么？
3. 选词：Please send us the **Bill of Loading / Lading** copy.
