# 外贸01 · 询盘与回复（Inquiry & Reply）

> 场景：海外买家通过邮件 / B2B 平台（Alibaba、Made-in-China）发来询盘
> 目标：看懂询盘类型、抓关键信息、24 小时内专业回复

## 一、询盘类型一眼识别

| 类型 | 特征词 | 买家意图 |
|---|---|---|
| General inquiry | catalog, price list, MOQ | 广撒网，先要资料 |
| Specific inquiry | quote for [具体型号], shipment to [港口] | 已有明确需求，比价阶段 |
| RFQ | request for quotation, send us your best price | 正式询价，通常量大 |
| Sample request | send us samples, sample cost | 接近下单，先验货 |

## 二、必背词块

**询盘里高频出现的词（看得懂）**：
- **MOQ** = minimum order quantity 最小起订量
- **lead time** 交期（从下单到发货的时间）
- **unit price / FOB price / CIF price** 单价 / 离岸价 / 到岸价
- **price list / quotation / quote** 报价单 / 报价
- **bulk order / trial order** 大批量订单 / 试单
- **terms of payment** 付款条件（T/T、L/C 见外贸03）

**回复时的标准动作词（用得出）**：
- Thank you for your **inquiry** about...
- Please find **attached** our quotation and catalog.
- We would be happy to **provide** you with samples.
- **Could you please confirm** your target quantity and destination port?
- We look forward to **your feedback**.

## 三、回复模板（可直接套用）

### 场景 A：对方只问了个大概（general inquiry）

```
Subject: Re: Inquiry about [产品]

Dear [Name],

Thank you for your inquiry about [产品/服务]. 

Please find attached our latest price list and product catalog. 
For your reference: our MOQ is [数量], and the lead time is [X] 
days after order confirmation.

Could you let us know your target quantity and destination port? 
We will then send you a formal quotation with the best price.

We look forward to hearing from you.

Best regards,
[你的名字]
[公司名]
```

### 场景 B：对方要具体型号报价（specific inquiry / RFQ）

```
Subject: Quotation for [型号] — [你的公司名]

Dear [Name],

Thank you for your specific inquiry. Based on your requirements, 
here is our offer:

- Product: [型号]
- Unit price: USD [X] (FOB [港口])
- MOQ: [数量]
- Lead time: [X] days
- Validity: This quotation is valid for [X] days.

We are also happy to send samples for your evaluation. 

Please don't hesitate to contact us if you have any questions.

Best regards,
[你的名字]
```

## 四、易错点（中文思维直译坑）

1. **"报价"别写 offer 统一**：一般询盘用 **quotation / quote**；offer 更常用于"报盘"（正式销售要约），日常邮件 quotation 最安全。
2. **"附件是"别写 attach 当动词**：正确是 **Please find attached...**（附件见…）或 **I've attached...**，不要说 "Attach is our quotation"。
3. **"期待回复"**：**We look forward to hearing from you.**（look forward to + doing）
4. **别直接甩报价单不说话**：先确认数量、港口、付款方式，再给正式报价——报价单带上下文才专业。

## 五、小练习

1. 翻译：请把你们的最低价和最小起订量发给我们。
2. 买家说 "We are just checking the market."，你怎么理解？要不要马上发正式报价？
3. 补全句子：Please find ______ our quotation. (attached / attach / attaching)
