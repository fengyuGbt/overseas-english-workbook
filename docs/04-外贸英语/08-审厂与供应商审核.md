# 外贸08 · 审厂与供应商审核（Factory Audit）

> 场景：客户或第三方（SGS/BV/TÜV）来厂审核
> 目标：知道审厂审什么、如何安排、审核发现问题后如何英文沟通整改

## 一、审厂类型（先分清对方来查什么）

| 类型 | 查什么 | 常见标准 |
|---|---|---|
| 质量审核 | 质量管理体系、过程控制 | ISO 9001 |
| 社会责任审核 | 工时、工资、消防、童工 | BSCI / SEDEX / SMETA |
| 反恐审核 | 门禁、监控、货物安全 | C-TPAT / GSV |
| 产能审核 | 产线、设备、订单排期能力 | 客户自定义 |

> 客户说 "we need to audit your factory" 时，**先问清类型和标准**：Could you confirm the audit standard and scope?

## 二、审厂审什么（Checklist）

**1. 资质与文件**
- Business license, ISO certificates, export qualifications
- 质量手册、程序文件、检验标准、员工培训记录

**2. 车间与设备**
- Production lines 产线布局、machine maintenance 设备保养记录
- Calibration 量具校准证书（卡尺、千分尺、测试仪器）

**3. 质量管理**
- IQC 来料检验（Incoming Quality Control）
- IPQC 制程检验（In-Process Quality Control）
- FQC/OQC 成品检验（Final/Outgoing QC）
- 不合格品隔离（non-conforming products quarantine area）

**4. 人员与环境**
- 员工培训、防护用品（PPE）
- 消防通道、灭火器、化学品存放

## 三、英语高频表达

**安排审厂：**
- We would like to **arrange a factory audit** on [日期].
- Please confirm your **audit agenda** and the number of auditors.
- We will **prepare the required documents** in advance.

**现场陪同（接待话术）：**
- Welcome to our factory. Let me **show you around** the production lines.
- This is our **incoming inspection area**; all raw materials are checked here.
- Here is our **quality control procedure**. Please feel free to ask any questions.
- Let me **explain our process flow** step by step.

**发现问题时（你要听懂并回应）：**
- We found a few **non-conformances** in [环节].
- You need to take **corrective actions** within [X] days.
- Please submit a **corrective action plan (CAP)**.

## 四、审核后整改邮件模板（CAP 回复）

```
Subject: Corrective Action Plan — Audit on [日期]

Dear [Name],

Thank you for the audit report dated [日期]. We have reviewed 
the findings carefully. Below is our corrective action plan:

1. Finding: [审核发现的问题]
   Root cause: [原因]
   Corrective action: [整改措施，如 retrain operators / 
   recalibrate equipment / revise the SOP]
   Completion date: [日期]

2. Finding: ...

We will submit photos and records as evidence once the actions 
are completed. Please let us know if you need any further 
information.

Best regards,
[你的名字]
```

## 五、易错点

1. **"审核"**：**audit**（审厂用 audit，不是 review/check）。**Auditor** = 审核员。
2. **"验厂通过"**：pass the audit / audit passed；不通过 = **failed the audit / non-conformance found**。
3. **"整改"**：**corrective action**；CAP = Corrective Action Plan。
4. **"陪同参观"**：**show you around** the factory（不是 "accompany you to see"）。
5. **"第三方审核"**：**third-party audit**（SGS, BV, TÜV）。

## 六、小练习

1. 翻译：我们想安排在 10 月 12 日进行一次工厂审核，请确认审核范围。
2. 审核员说 "There is no calibration record for the vernier calipers."，什么意思？你要怎么回？
3. 选词：We will take corrective actions **within / in** 7 days.
