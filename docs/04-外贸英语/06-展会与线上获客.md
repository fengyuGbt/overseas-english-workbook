# 外贸06 · 展会与线上获客（Exhibitions & Online Lead Gen）

> 场景：广交会/海外展会接待、B2B 平台询盘、LinkedIn 主动开发
> 目标：从"认识"到"成单"的英文沟通链路

## 一、展会现场（Trade Show）

**开场（主动破冰）：**
- Hi, welcome to our booth. **How can I help you today?**
- Are you **looking for** anything specific, or **just browsing**?
- May I have your **business card**? Here is mine.

**介绍产品（30 秒电梯稿）：**
- Our bestseller is [产品]. It's widely used in [行业/应用].
- Compared with competitors, our advantage is **better cost-performance / shorter lead time / custom design**.
- Here is a **live demo** — let me show you how it works.

**留线索（关键动作）：**
- Could you **leave your contact details** so we can send you a formal quotation?
- What is your **target quantity and destination**? I'll prepare the quote after the show.
- Let's **connect on LinkedIn / WhatsApp** for faster communication.

## 二、展后跟进邮件（48 小时内必发）

```
Subject: Great meeting you at [展会名] — [产品/服务]

Dear [Name],

It was a pleasure meeting you at [展会名] on [日期]. 
I really enjoyed learning about your [业务/市场].

As promised, please find attached:
1. Our quotation for [产品/型号]
2. Product catalog
3. Reference list of our clients in [国家/市场]

Regarding your question about [对方关注点], our solution is [回应].

Our offer is valid until [日期]. If you have any questions, 
feel free to reply to this email or reach me on WhatsApp [号码].

Looking forward to working with you.

Best regards,
[你的名字]
[公司名]
```

## 三、线上主动开发（Outbound）

**LinkedIn / cold email 第一封（短、价值前置）：**
```
Subject: [产品] for [客户行业] — quick question

Hi [Name],

I came across [公司名] and noticed you [具体观察：expanding 
into [市场] / importing [产品]].

We manufacture [产品] with [独特优势：e.g. 15-day lead time, 
ISO-certified factory]. 

Would it be helpful if I sent you our catalog and a reference 
price list? No obligation at all.

Best,
[你的名字]
```

**平台询盘回复（Alibaba 等）：** 参考外贸01 的询盘回复模板，外加：
- 先回平台内消息（12 小时内），再转 WhatsApp/邮件深聊
- 报价附件命名规范：`[产品]-[公司]-Quotation-[日期].pdf`

## 四、易错点

1. **"名片"**：**business card**，不是 name card（name card 也能懂但不地道）。
2. **"刚逛一逛"**：**just browsing / just looking around**。
3. **"发报价给你"**：**send you our quotation**（send + 双宾语），不是 "send to you our quotation"（口语化不正式）。
4. **"无义务"（cold email 必备）**：**no obligation at all**——降低对方防备心。
5. **"后续跟进"**：**follow up**（I'll follow up with you next week.），名词形式 follow-up。

## 五、小练习

1. 翻译：很高兴在广交会认识您。按约定，附件是我们的报价单和目录。
2. 展会上客户说 "We are just looking."，你怎么自然地继续对话？
3. 选词：Could you **send me / send to me** your catalog?
